ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Learn agriculture by doing it.

Prejonet Farmers Academy provides agricultural learning opportunities focused on practical skills and real farming environments. Our programmes cover animal production, plant production and focused short courses for people who want useful, hands-on farming knowledge.

Full-time students combine classroom theory with practical training, while online learners can complete the compulsory introduction and undertake work-integrated learning on a farm.

QUALIFICATION OPTIONS

National Certificate Programmes

The certificate courses require a Grade 10 certificate as a condition to apply.

NQF LEVEL 7

National Certificate: Animal Production

SAQA ID 48979

Designed for learners developing knowledge and practical capability in animal production.

NQF LEVEL 4

National Certificate: Animal Plant Production

SAQA ID 49009

A programme combining agricultural production knowledge with practical farming experience.

Full-time study

Monday to Friday, with approximately 30% theory and 70% practicals for each course.

Online study

A one-week compulsory introduction is followed by practical work. Students must find a farm for work-integrated learning for a period of one year and keep their logbook up to date.

Accommodation

Accommodation is available with three meals per day, Wi-Fi and laundry facilities.
